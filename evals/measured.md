Every held transfer above is releasable by the account holder in one tap once the cooling period elapses. The cost of a false positive is a minute; the cost of a false negative is the wallet.

**Read this figure carefully.** It is 10 hand-written cases against the stub Reader, not production traffic — the sample is far too small to quote as a real-world rate, and the corpus was written by the same people who wrote the rules it grades. It is a regression guard and an honest starting point, not a benchmark result.
